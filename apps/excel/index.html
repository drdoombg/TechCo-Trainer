Paste an export

This is what a real export puts on the clipboard: tab-separated, with trailing spaces, numbers stored as text, zero-padded codes and the odd blank row left in. It pastes at the current selection.

You can also just copy a block out of Excel, or anything tab-separated, and press Ctrl+V on the grid. Paste overwrites formulas. That is deliberate: it is how a re-paste into the wrong sheet quietly breaks a real workbook.
